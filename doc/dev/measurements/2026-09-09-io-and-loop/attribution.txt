starved/loop/disk/unexplained partition each interval's shortfall to
  ONE class, precedence work-starved > loop-bound > disk-wait, so the
  four columns sum to 100% of the shortfall.
D-thread bound: mean threads in D x the whole measured span, as a
  share of the shortfall. A thread in D is a thread not on a CPU, so
  this is the WHOLE budget the disk hypothesis has, and it needs no
  classification to be true -- only that the sampled censuses are
  representative of the intervals between them.
census cover: share of measured time carrying a thread census; the
  disk column can only see that much of the run.
